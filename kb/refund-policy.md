# Refund Policy & Eligibility

## 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
SupportFlow offers a full refund within 30 days of initial subscription purchase for new customers on all standard plans (Starter and Pro). If the platform does not meet your business operational needs within this 30-day window, you are entitled to a 100% refund of your first subscription payment.

## Refund Eligibility Conditions
Refunds are granted under the following conditions:
- **First-time subscriptions:** Requests submitted within 30 calendar days of initial sign-up.
- **Service disruption:** Documented technical downtime exceeding our 99.9% uptime SLA during any calendar month.
- **Billing errors:** Duplicate charges or incorrect invoice amounts are always refunded in full regardless of time frame.

## Non-Refundable Items
The following fees are strictly non-refundable:
- Enterprise custom implementation or onboarding engineering fees.
- Overage charges accrued for extra API calls or additional seat licenses consumed during the active billing period.
- Subscriptions renewed after the initial 30-day trial period, unless required by local statutory regulations.

## Processing Times
Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. We do not issue cash or physical check refunds.
