# Duplicate Charges Policy & Resolution

## Understanding Duplicate Charges
Duplicate charges most commonly occur when an initial payment transaction encounters a network latency or checkout timeout, causing the user to click the submit button multiple times. In other cases, a temporary pre-authorization hold from your bank appears alongside the settled transaction charge.

## Identifying Temporary Holds vs Settled Charges
- **Pre-authorization hold:** A temporary pending charge placed by your issuing bank to verify account funds. Holds automatically drop from your statement within 2 to 5 business days without deducting funds.
- **Settled duplicate charge:** Two identical debit charges with distinct transaction IDs that have both settled from 'Pending' to 'Posted'.

## How We Resolve Duplicate Charges
If you see two posted charges for the same billing period:
1. Contact support with your account email and the last four digits of the payment method.
2. Our billing team will verify whether two settled charges occurred.
3. If a duplicate settled charge is confirmed, we immediately void or refund the secondary charge.
4. Refunds typically reflect on your bank or credit card statement within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your financial institution.
