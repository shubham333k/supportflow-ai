# Data Export and Deletion Policy

## Workspace Data Export
Organization admins can export customer data, ticket histories, and analytics reports at any time:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Privacy & Compliance > Data Export**.
2. Select your desired format: **CSV** (for spreadsheets) or **JSON** (for programmatic ingest).
3. Select the desired date range or choose **All History**.
4. Click **Request Export**. A download link will be emailed to the primary admin within 15 minutes. Export archives are encrypted using AES-256 and expire after 72 hours.

## GDPR and CCPA Data Deletion Requests
In accordance with GDPR and CCPA regulations, customers may exercise their "Right to be Forgotten":
- **Individual Contact Deletion:** Open the contact profile in the dashboard, select **Actions > Delete Personal Data**, and confirm. All personal identifiable information (PII) including email, name, and ticket message contents is scrubbed immediately.
- **Full Workspace Deletion:** If an account is closed, all workspace data is retained in a dormant state for 30 days before irreversible destruction from primary databases and backup storage systems.
