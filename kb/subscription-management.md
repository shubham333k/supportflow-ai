# Subscription Management Guide

## Upgrading Your Plan
You can upgrade your subscription tier (for example, from Starter to Pro or Pro to Enterprise) at any point:
1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Plan Details**.
2. Click **Upgrade Plan** and select the desired tier.
3. The upgrade takes effect immediately.
4. Any unused time on your current billing cycle is credited as a prorated discount against the new plan invoice.

## Downgrading Your Plan
Downgrading to a lower plan tier:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Plan Details** and select **Change Plan**.
2. Confirm the feature limitations and reduced user seat allotments.
3. Downgrades take effect at the conclusion of your current billing period; your current tier features remain fully accessible until the renewal date.

## Canceling Your Subscription
To cancel an active subscription:
1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription**.
2. Follow the confirmation prompt and provide optional feedback.
3. You will retain full access until the end of the paid billing period. No further charges will occur.
4. You may export your workspace data at any time before your billing cycle concludes.
