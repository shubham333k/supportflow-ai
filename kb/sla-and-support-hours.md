# SLA Commitments & Support Operating Hours

## Support Coverage Hours
SupportFlow operations operate under the following standard business coverage:
- **Standard Operating Hours:** Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST / UTC-5).
- **Critical Incident Coverage:** 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year for Enterprise tier customers facing severe production outages or suspected security breaches.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA) Targets
Our first-response and resolution commitments are divided by customer tier and priority level:
- **Immediate Tier (Score ≥ 80 or Security/System Outage):** Guaranteed 1-hour first response time with dedicated engineering leadership.
- **Priority Tier (Score 60–79 or Billing Disputes):** Guaranteed 4-hour response time during business hours.
- **AI-Assisted Tier (Score 30–59):** Guaranteed 24-hour response time.
- **Automated Tier (Score < 30):** Guaranteed 48-hour response time for non-urgent inquiries.

## SLA Breach Monitoring
Our automated telemetry tracks timer progress continuously. At 50% and 90% of elapsed SLA time, alerts are routed to the on-call support team. If an SLA target is breached, tickets are automatically escalated with manager notifications.
